# Student Guide

## Buying And Access

- Browse marketplace catalog and trainer storefronts.
- Complete checkout from a course, video, bundle or subscription CTA.
- Open purchased content from the learning area.
- Review active access in the customer access center.

## Learning

- Track lesson completion and course progress.
- Submit homework/assignments where enabled.
- Use messaging to communicate with the trainer.
- Leave reviews after eligible purchases.

## Billing

- View purchases, subscriptions, payment statuses and receipts from billing pages.
- Refunded purchases may revoke content access.
- Expired subscriptions stop granting protected content access.
